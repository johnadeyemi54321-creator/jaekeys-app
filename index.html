Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

JaeKeys is made by Kehinde John Adeyemi, trading as Jaesoft, in Nigeria (“Jaesoft”, “we”). This policy explains what JaeKeys does with your information. We wrote it in plain English on purpose.

The short version

  • What you type stays on your phone. We do not collect, store or sell it.
  • JaeKeys has no ads, no tracking and no analytics.
  • A few features you choose to use send only the text needed for that feature to a trusted service, and only when you use them.

What stays on your phone

Your typing, learned words, shortcuts, clipboard items, saved details (like #bank or #address), stickers you make, your keyboard photo, settings and templates are stored only on your device. Clipboard items you do not pin are deleted after a short time. Uninstalling JaeKeys or clearing its storage deletes all of it.

About Android’s keyboard warning

When you turn on any keyboard, Android shows a standard warning that the keyboard “may be able to collect all the text you type”. Android shows this for every keyboard. JaeKeys does not collect your typing.

Features that use the internet (only when you use them)

  • Rewrite, Fix grammar and AI sentence check: the message or sentence is sent to an AI service (Groq, Google Gemini, or Pollinations as a backup) to return the improved text. Fix grammar may also use LanguageTool.
  • Translate: the text is sent to MyMemory to translate it.
  • GIFs: your GIF search words are sent to GIPHY (or Tenor, if that is what the keyboard uses).
  • Photo backgrounds: when you pick a photo look, the picture is downloaded from Unsplash. Nothing about you is sent.
  • Free unlock code: when you enter a code, the code and a random ID made by the app (not linked to your name or number) are sent to our unlock service to check it has not been used.
  • Support page: if you choose to support JaeKeys, the payment page opens in your browser and is run by Paystack. JaeKeys never sees your card details.
  • Money conversion: JaeKeys downloads exchange rates (open.er-api.com). No personal data is sent.
  • Quick search: your search opens in your browser, with the search engine you chose.
  • Voice typing: your voice is handled by your phone’s own speech service (usually Google), under that service’s privacy terms. With offline voice, it is turned into text on your phone and not sent anywhere.
  • Reviews and messages to John: only what you write (and any name or contact you add) is sent to us by email through Web3Forms. Review cards may be stored on imgbb so we can see them.
  • Update check: JaeKeys reads a small update file from GitHub.

Like any internet request, these services can see your device’s IP address. Each service has its own privacy policy. You can turn all of these off at once in Settings → AI features → Online features.

Photos and screenshots

If you allow it, JaeKeys looks at new screenshots on your phone only to offer them on the keyboard. It never uploads your photos.

Children

JaeKeys is meant for people aged 13 and over. If you are under 13, please use it only with a parent or guardian’s permission. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights

Because we do not hold your typing or personal data, there is usually nothing on our side to access or delete. If you sent us a message or review and want it removed, email us and we will delete it. We aim to follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and similar laws where you live.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when JaeKeys changes, and the new date will show at the top. Questions: jaekeys.app@gmail.com
